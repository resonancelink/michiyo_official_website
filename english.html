MICHIYO Spiritual Counsellor / Writer / Messenger

My name is Michiyo, a Japanese spiritual counsellor with approximately 20 years of experience. Working with the soul rather than the mind, I have provided readings for more than 7,000 individuals and organisations, offering deep insight and practical guidance on work, family, relationships and partnership.

I believe that the true purpose of spirituality is to remember, at the level of the soul, who you truly are, and to live and find happiness in a way that is authentically your own.

Today, my work centres on broadcasting and education. I share messages regularly through YouTube, Voicy, my blog, and my newsletter on Substack. I also teach through the Spiritual Meta-Science Academy, a school for those training to become spiritual counsellors, and Tokihana School, a course exploring creativity through AI.

I no longer offer general one-to-one sessions to the public. A limited number of sessions are available each month within my online salon.

Outside of my work, I design and create jewellery inspired by natural stones, and love progressive rock music and the roses I grow in my garden.
